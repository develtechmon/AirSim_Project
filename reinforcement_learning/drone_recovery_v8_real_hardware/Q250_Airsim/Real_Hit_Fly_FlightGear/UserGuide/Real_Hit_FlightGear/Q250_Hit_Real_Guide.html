1. What one hit does

You fly the drone up to 10 m and hold it in PosHold. You press two buttons. From that moment the Pi has the drone until it gives it back to you in PosHold, at the same spot and the same height, usually 5–15 s later.

A crash-test with a homing pigeon. The hit is the gust that throws it off course, the PPO is the bird getting its wings back, the height hold stops the fall, and then it flies home to exactly where it was before handing itself back to you. You stand there the whole time with your hand on the brake (CH8).

3. Safety layers (and what the "5 m" really is)

Like a fairground ride: a guard at the gate (checks before), seat belts during the ride (cut and rescue), the ride brings you back to the platform (fly-back), and the operator's big red button that works at any time (your CH8).
WhenLayerNumberWhat happens
Before (the 8 checks when a left button is pressed; any NO = REFUSED: … + 2 long beeps, the drone just keeps hovering)armed and flying—never hits a drone on the ground
your modePOSHOLD / LOITERthe Pi hands back to this mode
height above home≥ 8 mroom to fall (your hits lost up to 5.8 m)

Duringhit cut5 mif braking could not stop above 5 m, the hit stops early and the recovery starts
rescue3 mif it would pass 3 m: full-thrust rule until upright and climbing. Counts as FAILED
Afterguard A3 severy parameter put back and confirmed by the FC, else NOT CONFIRMED + 2 long beeps
fly-back2 m/s, 1.5 m/sback to the hit point and the hit height, then your PosHold
fly-back time-out30 snot back by then → handed to you anyway, 2 long beeps, back NO
AlwaysyouCH8move CH8 → the script lets go at once, whatever it is doing
The 5 m is a doorman, not a fence. It is only checked at the moment you press a left button: if the drone is more than 5 m (sideways) from where the script was started, that hit is refused and the drone keeps hovering in your PosHold. It never moves the drone, never does RTL, never lands. What brings the drone back after a hit is the fly-back (step 5), which is new in version e. Before version e the drone stayed wherever it had slid to.
What the fly-back does NOT do: it starts only once the drone is level again, so during the tumble and the braking the drone still slides (your run: up to 10.2 m on bird hard). It trusts GPS: real GPS noise and wind mean "back" will be within a metre or two, not 0.1 m. Keep about 15 m clear around the hit point, nobody in it.
